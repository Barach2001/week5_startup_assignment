# Kakuma Connect — 12-Slide Pitch Deck (First Draft)

**Founder:** Barach Aduot  
**Programme:** PLP  
**Stage:** Concept / first-draft proposal

## Slide 1 — Title
**Kakuma Connect** is a mobile-friendly platform to help people discover local services, businesses, jobs, learning opportunities and trusted community information in Kakuma.

## Slide 2 — Problem
- Useful information about local services and businesses can be scattered across different channels.
- Jobs, training, scholarships and announcements may be difficult to discover or search later.
- Old forwarded messages can be confusing and time-consuming to verify.
- Small local providers may have limited ways to reach potential customers.

## Slide 3 — Solution
One locally focused platform that organises service listings, opportunity notices and community updates. Users can browse categories, search, and contact providers through published details.

## Slide 4 — Product
- Searchable home page and categories.
- Local service and business directory.
- Opportunity board for jobs, training, scholarships and events.
- Dated community updates with sources and reporting options.
- Start with a mobile-friendly website; consider an app after testing.

## Slide 5 — Customers and market
- Primary users: Kakuma residents and young people seeking services, learning and work opportunities.
- Providers: small businesses, service providers, training centres and community organisations.
- Initial geography: Kakuma and nearby communities.
- Validate demand through interviews and pilot usage before claiming market size.

## Slide 6 — Business model
- Free basic browsing and basic listings.
- Optional paid, clearly labelled promoted listings.
- Potential partner service agreements for organisations sharing verified opportunities.
- Optional digital profile setup and promotion support.
- These are revenue hypotheses; no contracts or fees are claimed.

## Slide 7 — Competition
Alternatives include WhatsApp/social groups, noticeboards, word of mouth and general job or directory websites. Kakuma Connect's proposed difference is local focus, organised categories, dates, source links and community feedback. Validate the competitor landscape with users.

## Slide 8 — Go-to-market
1. Interview residents, youth groups and service providers.
2. Pilot a small directory with checked listings and opportunity sources.
3. Work with trusted community organisations, learning centres and businesses.
4. Improve the service using feedback and usage data.

## Slide 9 — Progress and validation
Current stage: concept / first-draft proposal. No product launch, user totals, revenue or partnerships are claimed. Milestones: discovery interviews, prototype, small pilot, then measurement of searches, successful contacts, repeat visits and listing accuracy.

## Slide 10 — Financial plan
Cost areas include domain/hosting, design and development tools, user research, content verification, moderation and support. Test optional promoted listings and partner agreements only after proving user value. Prepare a 12-month budget using real quotes; detailed forecasts are not invented in this draft.

## Slide 11 — Team
**Barach Aduot — Founder.** A technology and software-development learner interested in using digital tools to solve practical community problems. The project needs technical mentorship, user research support, community advisers and trusted information partners.

## Slide 12 — Ask and next steps
Seeking mentorship, feedback, pilot users and potential community partners.
- 30 days: interview users and prioritise categories.
- 60 days: build and test a simple prototype.
- 90 days: run a small pilot, review results and prepare a realistic budget.

**Vision:** Make useful local information easier to find, verify and act on.
