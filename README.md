## Startup concept
Kakuma Connect is a proposed mobile-friendly platform to organise local services, businesses, jobs, learning opportunities and community information for people in Kakuma.

## Important draft notes
This is a concept-stage first draft. It does not claim a launched product, customers, revenue, partnerships or measured market size. Validate demand through interviews and a pilot; replace assumptions with verified local data and actual supplier quotes.
